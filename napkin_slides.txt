WebMCP Launch Strategy: Lessons from Buyhatke

Slide 2 - Product-as-Distribution

Buyhatke's trick: no install needed. Type buyhatke.com/ before any Amazon link and you instantly see price history.




Lesson: The easier the first aha, the more people share it.
WebMCP: No SDK install - the webmcp.dev/demo link shows instant value.

Slide 3 - Radical Transparency + Creator Payments

When Amazon blocked their affiliate earnings, Buyhatke chose users over revenue: an open how-we-earn page and 100% of referral money to creators.




Lesson: Honesty is a marketing weapon; paying creators fully turns them into evangelists.
WebMCP: Publish benchmark methodology openly, pay developers rev-share.

Slide 4 - Multi-Vertical Retention

Price-drop alerts, auto-applied coupons, and a Spend Lens dashboard across shopping and flights keep users coming back. The longer they use it, the harder to leave.

Slide 5 - $0-Install Demo

Buyhatke saves shoppers money. WebMCP saves developers tokens: paste a link, see 79% less bytes and 42% fewer tokens on a 150-product benchmark. Instant proof, zero friction.

Slide 6 - Developer Ambassadors

Open-source contributors who verify benchmark numbers and promote WebMCP get micro-rewards and rev-share. Their public validation is free credibility.

Slide 7 - Token-Savings Recaps

Weekly recap to integrators: you saved 4.2M tokens this week. Weekly proof of value builds the habit of staying.

Slide 8 - 3-Week Launch Plan

Week 1: Launch the zero-install demo, publish methodology docs.

Week 2: Onboard developer ambassadors, ship the 150-product benchmark report.

Week 3: Cross-vertical expansion, automated token-savings recaps.

Slide 9 - The Scoreboard

Three KPIs:

Demo conversion rate

Active developer ambassadors

Deep integrations shipped

Slide 10 - Who Does What:

Developers: Claim rewards and build.

Browser vendors: Adopt the testing methodology.

Strategists: Watch live benchmarks.
